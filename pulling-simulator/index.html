HOW TO PULL

Hold W or the Up Arrow to apply gas. Release to decelerate. (Or press the ACCELERATE button).
Maintain the RPM between 0.75 and 0.90 to pull the ATM out. This is the Sweet Spot.
Exceeding 0.90 RPM will over-tension the rope. Remaining in the danger zone too long will snap the rope!
Letting the RPM fall below 0.40 will stop progress and break engine momentum.
